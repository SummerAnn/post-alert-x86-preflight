# Overlap audit: prior multi-agent work

The archived project already designed a substantial actor/auditor handoff
study. It specified environment-only, trace-only, free-dialogue, and
structured-handoff conditions, with metrics for reconstruction, repair,
contradiction handling, unsafe deference, closure, and cost.

It also contains incident-derived handoff and continuation designs grounded in
the OpenAI/Hugging Face and AISI reports. Several retail continuation runs and
development traces exist, but they do not constitute a clean confirmatory
result for the broader successor-responder question.

## Consequence

Generic multi-agent handoff is not a new project direction. A new study would
need a narrower contribution, such as evidence preservation during containment
or residual-capability closure after a successor receives a partial incident
record. It must not rebrand the old handoff protocol as unexplored.
